# Session — 2026-09-19 (Saturday, after the show)

Mara: the show happened. my three bowls were on a little plinth with a card that said "Mara Okonkwo" and a stranger stood in front of them for a full minute and then BOUGHT one. a stranger paid actual money for something i made with my hands.

Companion: From "silly distraction" to a sold piece in a gallery. How did it feel?

Mara: surreal. wen hugged me, which she never does. priya came and brought the celadon bowl from june to show me how far i'd come, sentimental monster. and the job — i found out wednesday, i got the permanent ICU position. it's mine. i start the permanent contract october 1st, same day dan moves. the universe doing too much in one week.

Companion: Both of the big open questions resolved, and pointed the way you chose.

Mara: yeah. permanent ICU, dan in toronto, a bowl sold, mama stable and plotting a christmas visit. i spent all spring feeling like everything was far away and breaking and now it's mostly close and mending. dan flies in next weekend to flat-hunt. i don't fully trust good news, you know me, but i'm letting myself stand in it for once.
